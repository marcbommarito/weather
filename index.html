      Configured numeric thresholds

      Wind, heat-index and precipitation thresholds are local operational settings and should be reviewed and formally adopted by the district. NWS HeatRisk and AQI categories retain their source-system meanings.

      Important limitations

      • No free public feed in this package provides reliable real-time lightning-strike distance. Add a licensed lightning provider before using the dashboard for a “clear after last strike” countdown.
      • Airport and agricultural stations may not represent conditions at every school site. Staff should verify unusual local conditions.
      • A missing or stale feed is displayed as unavailable; it is never treated as a safe condition.
      • This dashboard supports, but does not replace, the district’s adopted SB 1248 protocol, emergency plans, or authorized judgment.

      Sources: National Weather Service, EPA AirNow, California DWR CIMIS, and district-configured thresholds.
